          • £1,053average fire safety spend per UK business, every year
          • £3,000of fire safety per person over a lifetime, about £87 a year
          • £20.7mif just 1% of the UK bought one £30 fire product

          Put simply: the UK spends £6bn a year on fire safety. That works out at about £1,053 per business, or £87 per person every year — around £3,000 each over a lifetime. Most products cost about £30, so if just 1% of the UK bought one, that alone is £20.7m of sales. The market is huge because these are small, repeat purchases that the law requires.

            07 The £100 Million Freedom Global Ambition

            From ecommerce business to global brand platform

            The ten-year vision is not simply to sell more fire extinguishers. It is to build the infrastructure capable of launching, owning and scaling multiple consumer brands.

            2031 → 2036 implies ~31% a year

            2036 revenue mix, £100.59m

            Hover or tap a segment or legend row

              How the mix shifts

              Channel share of revenue, eBay seeds it; Amazon, Temu & B2B scale it

              eBay: ~60% of 2026 revenue → 22% by 2030 → 10% by 2036, while absolute eBay revenue still grows ~6.5×. That’s diversification, not decline.

              The conditions

              What the £100 million ambition assumes

              A long term value creation target, not a current valuation. Reaching it depends on all of the following holding true.

              1. Channel executionAmazon, Shopify, OnBuy and TikTok Shop all launch and scale on plan, alongside eBay and Temu.
              2. Working capitalStock funding keeps pace with demand, so availability and marketplace ranking are never the constraint.
              3. Brand portfolioThe owned brands repeat the Firestorm model across batteries, PPE, aerosols, cleaning and automotive.
              4. Margin disciplineDirect-from-manufacturer sourcing holds gross margin as volume grows.
              5. Operational capacityWarehousing, fulfilment and headcount scale ahead of order volume, not behind it.
              6. Market conditionsThe continued migration of regulated safety and consumable categories online.

              £100 million is a target enterprise value, an illustrative future figure based on successful execution of the plan above. It is not a professional valuation and should not be read as one.
